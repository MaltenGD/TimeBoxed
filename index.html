What is TimeBoxed?

TimeBoxed is a unique cat-like adventure that combines the strategic depth of board games with the thrill of time travel. In this journey you will go through different eras, and battle different mythological gods

Game Objective

The main objective of the game is to recover your beloved yarn ball. To do this, they must help Kronos retrieve three lost objects by winning games against various mythological gods across different eras.
